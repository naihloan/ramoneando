## Why I Read This
I wanted to streamline my workflow and reduce the mental overhead of managing multiple projects...

## Key Takeaways
1. **Capture everything:** If it's on your mind, it's not getting done.
2. **Next Actions:** Don't list projects; list the very next physical step.
3. **The 2-Minute Rule:** If it takes less than two minutes, do it now.
