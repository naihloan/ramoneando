* Find 3 notes
    * You can use use 1 3 4
    * More:
        * add a blue note: 1/2 step after the 4th 
        * add a 5th 
        * ~~use a only [pentatonic or major scale if you're looking for more](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/TjBHejWrDK8). not required~~
* Stick to those notes
* Find the important note, the 1 --or root-- and go back to it to mark phrases
* Leave some space (silence), and bends 
* Repeat
* If in doubt to jam: 
    * [ask](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EIx0MNOAZI4)
    * [or explain what you're doing](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/EIx0MNOAZI4)
